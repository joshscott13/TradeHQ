## Catalog and common constraints

The [official help catalog](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/) lists Flex, Pro, Daily, Black (explicitly legacy), Direct (straight to funded) and Maxx. Flex/Pro/Daily are ordinary evaluation-to-simulated-funded families. Black is an existing legacy family; its pages do not establish current purchasability or an exact legacy purchase cutoff. Direct is excluded.

There is one additional non-direct evaluation route: [Maxx overview](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13891785-lucidmaxx-overview) describes an invite-only evaluation-to-live program, not simulated funding. Its [evaluation rules](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/14315460-lucidmaxx-eval-rules) include `$50k`, `$3,000` target, `$2,000` loss limit, 40% consistency and five trading days. Passing goes directly live. Do not incorrectly classify it as Direct or ordinary public funded planning; defer unless Maxx eligibility and live scope are specifically selected.

[Account limits](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404617-maximum-number-of-accounts) permit five active funded accounts per household/family across funded types, ten combined evaluation/funded accounts, and five live accounts. New homogeneous scenarios should use 1–5 funded accounts with an explicit assumption that no other household accounts occupy slots. Existing Flex's 1–100 scenario range is documented as unchecked hypothetical scaling; changing it requires a follow-up contract.

Gross request, trader share and retained profit are different quantities. The following monetary request amounts precede the split. Daily trading profit's precise commission basis, official cent rounding and gross-request balance accounting are not comprehensively defined by these pages. Proposed arithmetic deducts full gross request from retained profit, models 90% as trader cash and does not subtract commissions twice; that remains a declared modeling convention pending actual reconciliation.

## Contradictions and unresolved policy fields

- **Pro caps:** funded overview states no simulated payout caps, while the dedicated payout table gives numerical request caps. This could mean no aggregate lifetime profit cap, but that explanation is an inference. Obtain a dated cohort-specific resolution before presenting a policy as settled.
- **Pro live cohorts:** [legacy live](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13432107-lucidpro-live-legacy) expressly covers purchase/reset on February 27, 2026 and prior and lists sixth final payout; it also distinguishes January 19 and prior transition terms. [Current live structure](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13425130-new-live-structure) uses final Payout 5 and discretionary earlier review. Do not collapse split and live cutoff dates into a single legacy flag.
- **Daily live boundary:** payout page describes automatic transition at `$8,000` daily simulated profit; [Daily live](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/16010520-luciddaily-live) calls that a review-pool trigger and says transitions are discretionary. Other triggers—significant lifetime payouts, exceptional performance, prior live history—have no numerical cutoff. No official finite payout-count maximum for Daily was found. Its live page caps possible simulated-profit-above-buffer settlement at `$15,000` across the trader's accounts; buffer funds live drawdown and cannot be withdrawn. Exclude all such settlement/live income from a funded-only prototype.
- **Current portfolio closure:** the current general live structure and Daily live FAQ both say all simulated accounts close on transition; eligible funded accounts with at least one payout move live. Both also prohibit household members from trading simulated accounts while a member is live. Thus simulated-funded accounts cannot be projected as independent recurring cash streams after another account's transition. This current scope must not overwrite the separately identified legacy Pro/Black transition contracts.
- **Flex freshness:** [payout rules](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945796-lucidflex-payouts) still list five separate days at least `$150`, positive cycle net profit, `$500` minimum, 50% profit up to `$2,000`, 90% share and five payouts. [Drawdown](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945815-lucidflex-drawdown) still locks MLL to `$50,100` at request. Current general live page qualifies transition as discretionary and possibly earlier. Preserve existing ADR 0006/code; flag the fifth-payout stop as its modeled boundary, not assurance of five approvals or automatic live date.
- **Common unknowns:** official rounding, exact consistency commission basis, processing holds/pending overlaps, payout gross/net balance evidence, source session timezone/DST semantics, account history and discretionary selection remain unresolved. Positive end-of-day results do not establish no intraday breach. No real records or interviews were collected.

## Proposed next safe scope and manual examples

**Recommendation, awaiting a new accepted ADR:** a fresh LucidDaily $50k **first-request-only** scenario with constant loss-free daily net profit strictly below `$8,000`, 1–5 identical funded accounts, no other household accounts/history, immediate hypothetical approval and no transfers/fees. Pause after first request and separate unmodeled later days from earned cash. This is a narrow modeling convention, not an official one-payout cap, live trigger or guarantee the account survives until request. It is insufficient for full monthly/yearly side-income planning: later cycles, pending timing and discretionary live stop need their own explicit contract. Pro remains conflict-blocked; Black is coherent for an explicitly legacy synthetic scenario but current availability/cohort identification and standard-versus-bonus strategy need decisions.
